## What's new

- **Shapes land exactly on your stroke on pages made for another device.** An A5X/A6X note opened on a Manta (or the other way round) made shapes about 37 % too large and shifted. Thanks to **@soerennielsen** for reporting it and pointing to the page size.
- **Wide, flat rectangles are no longer mistaken for triangles.**
- **Centre dot for circles** (off by default, in the circle's settings), handy for unit circles.

Install: copy `Snap.snplg` to `MyStyle`, then **Settings → Apps → Plugins → Add plugin**. Your settings are kept.
